## About

I run DAASK, a data and AI consultancy based in Nairobi, founded in March 2025. We build dashboards, data pipelines, and AI automation systems for organizations that need real operational tools, not just reports.

Before DAASK, I was Lead Data Analyst at Tripitaca (Triply.co, Y Combinator-backed), where I partnered directly with the CEO and executive leadership to build a unified executive dashboard in Tableau and Power BI. Before that, I spent four years at Forte Rapida Freight moving from Business Development Officer and Fleet Operations Lead into Business Development Manager, and I was a Creative Tech Resident Consultant at The GIANT Room.

BA in International Relations, USIU-Africa. Diploma in Business & IT, Strathmore University.
